Quarterly review

The team shipped the new analytics platform two weeks ahead of schedule. Customers report that the dashboards are fast, reliable and easy to use, and the support
queue is the shortest it has been all year. We are proud of the progress and excited about the opportunities in the next quarter.

There were problems as well. The migration of the old reporting system caused delays, and a few customers complained about errors in exported files.
The risk of further delays remains, and the budget is not as strong as we had hoped. Dr. Weber estimates that the fixes need about 3.5 weeks.

Next steps: finish the migration, close the open complaints and review the plan with the board.
